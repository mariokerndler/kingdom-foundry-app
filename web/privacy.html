What this app stores

Kingdom Foundry stores your app preferences, selected expansions, rules, disabled cards, language preference, and local kingdom history in your browser storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us by the app.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Hostinger. When you visit the site, technical log data such as your IP address, browser information, requested files, date, and time may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the website and keep it secure.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or non-essential tracking cookies. Browser storage is used only for app functionality.

Legal basis

Local app storage is used to provide requested app functionality. Hosting logs are processed for website delivery, security, and operation.

Retention

Local app data remains in your browser until you clear site data or reset the app. Hosting log retention is controlled by Hostinger and should be checked against your hosting configuration or data processing agreement.

Your rights
